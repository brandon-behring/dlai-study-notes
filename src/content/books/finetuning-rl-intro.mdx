# Fine-tuning & RL for LLMs: Intro to Post-training

Personal study notes on the [DLAI short course](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/fine-tuning-rl-for-llms-intro-to-post-training/)
taught by **Sharon Zhou** in partnership with **AMD**.

The course is the broader, newer Sharon Zhou course — bridging supervised
fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement-learning post-training (DPO, GRPO,
RLHF-family). For deeper coverage of preference optimization specifically,
see the **Post-training of LLMs** (NexusFlow) and **GRPO Fine-tuning**
(Predibase) sibling courses in this corpus.
