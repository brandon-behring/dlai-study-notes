# Evaluating AI Agents

Study notes on the [DLAI short course](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/evaluating-ai-agents/)
taught by **John Gilhuly** and **Aman Khan** in partnership with **Arize AI** —
how to tell whether an LLM agent actually works. Why non-determinism breaks
traditional testing, how to decompose an agent into router / skills / memory,
instrument it with traces, and evaluate each component, the trajectory, and the
end-to-end task — including LLM-as-a-judge and production monitoring.

These notes are the **evaluation** half of a pair: the
[Fine-tuning & RL for LLMs](/finetuning-rl-intro/) guide covers how to *train* a
model; this one covers how to *measure* whether it works. Together they span the
train-and-evaluate loop interviewers probe at AI-engineering roles.
