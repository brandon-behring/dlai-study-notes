# Advanced RAG

A readable online companion to the [DeepLearning.AI Building and Evaluating
Advanced RAG short
course](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/building-evaluating-advanced-rag/),
taught by **Jerry Liu** (LlamaIndex) and **Anupam Datta** (TruEra).

The course teaches advanced retrieval strategies and how to evaluate them
systematically using the RAG triad: Context Relevance, Groundedness, and
Answer Relevance.
