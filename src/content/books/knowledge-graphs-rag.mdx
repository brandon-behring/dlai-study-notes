# Knowledge Graphs for RAG

Study notes on the [DLAI short course](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/knowledge-graphs-rag/)
taught by **Andreas Kollegger** in partnership with **Neo4j** — using a knowledge
graph to retrieve by *connection*, not just by *similarity*. Graph data modelling
(nodes, relationships, properties, labels), the Cypher query language, preparing
text for RAG with vector indexes, building and expanding a knowledge graph from
SEC filings, and answering questions by having an LLM generate Cypher against the
graph.

This is the **retrieval** guide in the set: the
[Fine-tuning & RL](/finetuning-rl-intro/) guide covers training a model and
[Evaluating AI Agents](/evaluating-ai-agents/) covers proving a system works;
this one covers grounding an LLM in structured, connected knowledge.
