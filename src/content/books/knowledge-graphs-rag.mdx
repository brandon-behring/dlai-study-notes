# Knowledge Graphs for RAG

A readable online companion to the [DeepLearning.AI Knowledge Graphs for RAG
short course](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/knowledge-graphs-rag/),
taught by **Andreas Kollegger** in partnership with **Neo4j**.

The course is video + handout format; these notes are the written synthesis
form that didn't otherwise exist — paraphrased concepts, learning outcomes,
margin notes for interview prep, worked problems, and Anki flashcards.

## Compliance

## What's in here

Seven chapters synthesizing the course material:

1. Knowledge Graph Fundamentals — nodes, relationships, labels, properties
2. Querying Knowledge Graphs — Cypher pattern matching
3. Preparing Text for RAG — vector indexes on graph data
4. Constructing a Knowledge Graph from Text Documents — SEC 10-K parsing
5. Adding Relationships to the SEC Knowledge Graph — chunk linking
6. Expanding the SEC Knowledge Graph — Form 13 investor data
7. Chatting with the Knowledge Graph — LLM-generated Cypher
