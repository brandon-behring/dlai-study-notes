Ten steps into training, the loss jumps to `NaN`. What is the first thing to
change?

<Rationale title="Why">
**Reduce the learning rate ~10×.** `NaN` loss is the signature of an
LR-too-high blow-up: a step overshoots, weights explode, the loss becomes
undefined. Training more epochs just runs the broken dynamics longer; raising the
LR makes it worse; swapping the optimizer doesn't address the step size. The
reflex from the loss-curve diagnosis table: NaN → LR too high → cut 10×.
</Rationale>
