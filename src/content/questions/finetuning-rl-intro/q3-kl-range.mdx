During RL you monitor KL divergence from the reference policy. It has climbed past
**0.6 nats** and rollouts are converging on one phrasing. What does this most likely
indicate?

<Rationale title="Why">
**Reward hacking / mode collapse.** The safe KL band is ~0.1–0.2 nats; above ~0.5 the
policy has strayed far from the reference, and collapsing rollout diversity is the
tell-tale signature of gaming the reward. Reading high KL as "healthy exploration" has
it backwards. *Lowering* β would loosen the leash and let it drift further. Underfitting
is the opposite regime — KL near or below 0.1, where RL is barely moving the model.
</Rationale>
