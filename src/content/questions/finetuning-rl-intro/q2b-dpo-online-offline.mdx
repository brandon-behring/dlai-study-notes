What is the **core practical difference** between DPO and the PPO/GRPO family?

<Rationale title="Why">
**DPO is offline, PPO/GRPO are online.** DPO trains once on a fixed
`chosen`/`rejected` dataset with no reward model and no sampling loop; PPO and GRPO
generate fresh rollouts from the current policy each step and score them live. (b)
is backwards — it's DPO that *skips* the reward model. (c) misuses the on/off-policy
terms. (d) conflates DPO with the PPO/GRPO critic distinction. The online/offline
axis is the clean way to file these algorithms.
</Rationale>
