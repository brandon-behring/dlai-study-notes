answer: >
  Start small — about 20 to 50 examples. The model already knows the content; you are only
  changing the output shape, which sits at the bottom of the SFT scale ladder where
  formatting and structure are learned. Spending thousands of examples here wastes
  annotation budget and risks overfitting to your exact phrasings, which can degrade the
  model's general ability. Fine-tune on the small set, evaluate, and add more only if the
  format is not reliably followed. Common wrong answer: "a few thousand, to be safe" — that
  confuses changing behavior with changing format, and over-trains a problem that 20 to 50
  demonstrations usually solve.
