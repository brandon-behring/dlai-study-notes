Why can a freshly pre-trained **base model** not be deployed as a chat assistant
as-is?

<Rationale title="Why">
**(b).** Pre-training *does* give the base model broad world knowledge — that is
exactly what (a) and (d) get wrong. What it lacks is *behavior*: following
instructions, refusing unsafe requests, and producing structured reasoning.
Post-training (SFT + RL) installs those. (c) is about serving cost, not why the
base model is behaviorally undeployable.
</Rationale>
