Why can a freshly pre-trained **base model** not be deployed as a chat assistant
as-is?

<Rationale title="Why">
Pre-training **already gives** the base model broad world knowledge and trained
(not random) weights — exactly what the "lacks world knowledge" and "randomly
initialized" options get wrong. What it lacks is *behavior*: following
instructions, refusing unsafe requests, and producing structured reasoning, all
installed by post-training (SFT + RL). The "too large to serve" option is about
serving cost, not why the base model is behaviorally undeployable.
</Rationale>
