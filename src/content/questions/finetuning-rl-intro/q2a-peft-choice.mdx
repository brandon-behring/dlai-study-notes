You must fine-tune a **7B** model on a single **12 GB** consumer GPU. Which
approach gives the **most memory headroom** while keeping near-full-fine-tuning
quality?

<Rationale title="Why">
**QLoRA.** Full fine-tuning needs ~60 GB (weights + grads + Adam states), and
gradient checkpointing trims only *activations* — nowhere near enough. fp16 LoRA
freezes the base but still loads ~14 GB of 16-bit weights, already over 12 GB. The
8-bit-base option is the genuine runner-up — it *can* fit — but QLoRA's 4-bit NF4
base (~4 GB for the weights) leaves the **most** headroom for batch size and
context with minimal quality loss. It's the standard single-consumer-GPU recipe.
</Rationale>
