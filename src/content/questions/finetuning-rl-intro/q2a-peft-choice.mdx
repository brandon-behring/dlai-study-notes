You must fine-tune a **7B** model on a single **12 GB** consumer GPU. Which
approach fits?

<Rationale title="Why">
**QLoRA.** Rough memory: full fine-tuning of a 7B model needs ~60 GB and
fp16 LoRA still needs ~16 GB — both exceed 12 GB. QLoRA stores the frozen base in
4-bit NF4 (~6 GB for 7B) with full-precision adapters, fitting comfortably with
minimal quality loss. (d) is wrong precisely because QLoRA exists to make this
case work; it's the standard answer for sub-16 GB single-GPU fine-tuning.
</Rationale>
