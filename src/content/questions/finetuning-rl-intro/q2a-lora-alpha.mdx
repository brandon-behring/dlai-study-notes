answer: >
  The LoRA update is scaled by α/r, so it is the *ratio* that sets adaptation
  magnitude. Going r=8→16 while holding α=16 changes the ratio from 16/8=2 to
  16/16=1 — you've silently *halved* the effective update strength, so the more
  expressive higher-rank adapter may actually adapt *less*, confounding your
  experiment. Fix: scale α with r (set α=32 to keep α/r=2), or use a
  rank-stabilized variant. Common wrong answer: "higher rank always means stronger
  adaptation" — true for capacity, false for magnitude once α/r drops.
