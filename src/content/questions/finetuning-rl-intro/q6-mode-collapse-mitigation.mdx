During RL, the policy has collapsed to a single high-scoring output template —
the judge reward is near-maximal but the outputs are repetitive and low-quality.
Which is the **most direct** mitigation?

<Rationale title="Why">
**A KL penalty to the SFT reference.** Mode collapse is the policy drifting into
a narrow, reward-gaming region; penalizing divergence from the diverse SFT
baseline pulls it back toward varied behavior. Options (b) and (c) *accelerate*
the hack — a stronger or longer-optimized broken reward collapses harder.
Optimizer choice (d) is irrelevant to specification gaming. (Diversity
monitoring would *detect* the collapse; the KL penalty *prevents* it.)
</Rationale>
