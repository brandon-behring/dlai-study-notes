Your LLM judge fails meta-evaluation. Which improvement lever should you reach for *first*, and
why?

<Rationale title="Why">
Roughly 80% of judge failures are prompt failures — vague criteria or a loose output format —
so reworking the prompt is the cheapest, highest-yield first move. A larger model targets
capability gaps, which are the *less* common cause; semantic similarity fixes a specific
failure (paraphrase false-negatives), not a general low score; and averaging runs addresses
variance, not the systematic mis-judgment a failed meta-evaluation reveals. Apply one lever at
a time and re-meta-evaluate.
</Rationale>
