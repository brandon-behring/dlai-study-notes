Tool selection is 90% and parameter extraction is 80%, on independent dimensions. What is the
end-to-end router accuracy, and why is it below both?

<Rationale title="Why">
Both independent steps must succeed, so the rates multiply: $0.90 \times 0.80 = 0.72$. The
product falls below either factor because each step discards some of the other's successes — it
is neither their average (85%), nor capped at the lower factor (80%), nor dominated by the
higher one (90%). This is the multiplicative-accuracy effect, and it's why you fix the weaker
dimension first.
</Rationale>
