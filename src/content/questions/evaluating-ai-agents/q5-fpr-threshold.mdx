answer: >
  Agreement rate is the overall match with ground truth; the false-positive rate isolates one
  direction — wrong answers the judge passes as correct (leniency) — and the false-negative rate
  the other (strictness). They differ because a high agreement can still hide many false
  positives if errors cluster on the rarer class, and for a *gate*, leniency is the dangerous
  error: a lenient judge ships bad answers. Pick both thresholds: require overall agreement ≥ 85%
  AND FPR ≤ 10% (tighten the FPR cap further for safety-critical gates). Reject the judge if
  either fails. Common wrong answer: "agreement ≥ 90% is enough" — it can coexist with a high FPR,
  so a single threshold lets a lenient judge through.
