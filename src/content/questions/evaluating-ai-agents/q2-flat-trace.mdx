Your trace is one flat span for the whole run. To localise a misrouted tool call, which manual
spans most directly restore the needed structure?

<Rationale title="Why">
Adding Agent, Chain (per router iteration), and Tool (per skill) spans rebuilds the router and
tool boundaries, so a misroute localises to a Chain span and a bad tool result to its Tool
span. A lone LLM span around the answer, or one Tool span wrapping everything, leaves the trace
effectively flat; metric counters give totals but no structure to navigate, which is exactly
what debugging a misroute needs.
</Rationale>
