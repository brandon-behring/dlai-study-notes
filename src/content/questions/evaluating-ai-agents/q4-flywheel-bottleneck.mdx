What is the most common bottleneck in the production feedback flywheel?

<Rationale title="Why">
The flywheel is only as fast as its slowest stage, and that stage is usually turning a
production trace into a dataset case: if it takes a week of manual review, the loop stalls and
improvements lag reality. Production run cost and judge model size affect spend or latency, not
the loop's *iteration speed*, and more evaluators add scoring work but aren't the structural
chokepoint — promotion speed is, which is why teams automate it (with a quality gate).
</Rationale>
