In an OpenTelemetry-instrumented agent, what is the relationship between a trace and a span?

<Rationale title="Why">
A trace is the end-to-end record of one run; a span is a single nested step, and spans form a
tree under the trace sharing one trace ID. Swapping the two inverts the containment. They are
not interchangeable names for one model call — a single call is one LLM *span*, not a whole
trace. And both record timing; a span captures inputs, attributes, and status, not merely a
final output.
</Rationale>
