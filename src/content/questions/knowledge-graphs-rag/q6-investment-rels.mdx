answer: >
  Two relationship types bridge the datasets. FILED connects Company to Form, created by matching
  on the shared cusip6 — `MATCH (com:Company), (f:Form) WHERE com.cusip6 = f.cusip6 MERGE
  (com)-[:FILED]->(f)`. OWNS_STOCK_IN connects Manager to Company, created per CSV row by matching
  both endpoints on their keys and MERGE-ing the edge with shares, value, and quarter — and because
  it is MERGE'd on `reportCalendarOrQuarter`, the same manager-company pair can hold one edge per
  reporting period. FILED is the document bridge (10-K data); OWNS_STOCK_IN is the investment
  bridge (Form 13 data). Common wrong answer: a single edge per manager-company pair, which would
  overwrite quarter-by-quarter history.
