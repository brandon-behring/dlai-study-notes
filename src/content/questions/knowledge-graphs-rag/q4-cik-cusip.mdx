In an SEC Form 10-K pipeline, what role do CIK and CUSIP play?

<Rationale title="Why">
CIK (Central Index Key) and CUSIP are *identifiers*: the CIK keys a company across all its SEC
filings, and the CUSIP (first 6 digits) identifies the issuer across datasets — so they become
the linking keys when the graph gains relationships. They are not text sections (those are Item
1/1A/7/7A), not vector-index parameters (dimensions and similarity function), and not LangChain
chain types.
</Rationale>
