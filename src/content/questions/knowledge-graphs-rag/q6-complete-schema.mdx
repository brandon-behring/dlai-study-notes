answer: >
  Four node types — Chunk (text + embedding), Form (the 10-K), Company, Manager. Five relationship
  types — Chunk NEXT Chunk (linked list), Chunk PART_OF Form (membership), Form SECTION Chunk
  (section entry), Company FILED Form (filing, via CUSIP), Manager OWNS_STOCK_IN Company
  (investment, with shares/value/quarter). Two indexes — a vector index on Chunk.textEmbedding and
  a full-text index on Manager.managerName. Together these support semantic search (vector),
  keyword search (full-text), sequential and hierarchical document navigation (NEXT/PART_OF/
  SECTION), and cross-entity traversal (FILED/OWNS_STOCK_IN). Common wrong answer: forgetting the
  indexes — they are part of the schema's capability, not an afterthought.
