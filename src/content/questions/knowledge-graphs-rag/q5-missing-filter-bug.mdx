answer: >
  Count the NEXT edges per section, not across the whole form. With 10 chunks split 0–4
  (Introduction) and 5–9 (Methods), correct creation gives 4 edges in each section (0→1→2→3→4 and
  5→6→7→8→9) = 8 total. The buggy version that orders all chunks by chunkSeqId without the section
  filter adds a ninth edge, 4→5, linking the last Introduction chunk to the first Methods chunk —
  a cross-section link. Detect it by querying for NEXT edges whose endpoints have different `item`
  values: `MATCH (a)-[:NEXT]->(b) WHERE a.item <> b.item RETURN a, b`. Any rows are boundary
  bleed. Fix by deleting those edges and rebuilding with the item filter.
