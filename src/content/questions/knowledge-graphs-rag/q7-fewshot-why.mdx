Why inject the graph schema and a "do not hallucinate" instruction into a Cypher-generation prompt?

<Rationale title="Why">
The schema plus the instruction constrain the LLM to *real* relationship types and properties,
preventing schema hallucination — Cypher that looks valid but references things the graph doesn't
have. It doesn't change execution speed, doesn't remove the need for examples, and doesn't bypass
running the query.
</Rationale>
