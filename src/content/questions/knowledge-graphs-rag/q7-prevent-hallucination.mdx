An LLM generates Cypher referencing a relationship type that doesn't exist in your graph. Which fix
most directly addresses this?

<Rationale title="Why">
Schema hallucination is fixed by grounding the model in the real schema and checking its output:
inject the actual node/relationship types, instruct "use only these", and validate before
executing. Raising temperature makes invention *more* likely, removing the schema removes the
guardrail, and the search paradigm is unrelated to the generation step.
</Rationale>
