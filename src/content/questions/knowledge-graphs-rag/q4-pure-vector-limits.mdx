answer: >
  After this chapter the chunks are embedded but disconnected — Neo4j is functionally just a
  vector store. So you cannot follow a chunk back to its source filing, cannot retrieve the
  adjacent chunk for expanded context, and cannot traverse from a chunk to related entities (the
  company that filed it, other filings, investors). All three need relationships, which Module 5
  adds as NEXT (chunk order), PART_OF (chunk to form), and SECTION links. Common wrong answer:
  "similarity search is broken" — vector search works fine; what is missing is *connection*, not
  retrieval.
