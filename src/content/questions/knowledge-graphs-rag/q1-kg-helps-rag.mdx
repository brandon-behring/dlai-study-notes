Why does a knowledge graph enhance RAG *beyond* vector similarity search?

<Rationale title="Why">
The graph adds a *retrieval mode* embeddings lack: traversal of typed relationships, which
reaches entities *connected* to a result (a company's investors, a chunk's neighbouring
sections) rather than merely *similar* text. It isn't about compressing embeddings, it doesn't
swap semantic search for keyword matching, and it doesn't remove the LLM — generation still
happens; the graph just supplies richer, connection-aware context.
</Rationale>
