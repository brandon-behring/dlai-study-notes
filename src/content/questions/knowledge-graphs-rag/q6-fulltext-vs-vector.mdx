answer: >
  A full-text index matches *strings* — exact, partial, and fuzzy keyword search with relevance
  scoring (e.g. find managers matching "Royal Bank"). A vector index matches *meaning* — embeddings
  whose semantics are close, even when the words differ. Use full-text when the query is a name or
  keyword whose spelling matters; use vector when you want conceptually similar content regardless
  of wording. They are complementary, and Neo4j adds a third — property indexes for exact lookup by
  a known id. Common wrong answer: "full-text is just a slower vector search" — they answer
  different questions (spelling vs meaning), not the same question at different speeds.
