answer: >
  Four, each suited to a different question. Vector search — semantic similarity for conceptual
  questions ("tell me about cloud storage"). Full-text search — keyword/string lookup for entities
  ("find Royal Bank of Canada"). Graph traversal — relationship following for structural questions
  ("who are NetApp's investors?"). Geospatial search — distance with point.distance() for location
  questions ("companies within 10 km of San Jose"). No single paradigm answers every question; the
  finished graph's power is holding all four in one engine, and complex questions compose them.
  Common wrong answer: treating vector search as a catch-all — it finds meaning, not exact names,
  relationships, or distances.
