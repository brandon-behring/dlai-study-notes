answer: >
  Section filtering is a correctness requirement because every section restarts chunkSeqId at 0,
  and NEXT links chunks whose sequence ids differ by one. Filter only on formId and order by
  chunkSeqId — without the item filter — and the last chunk of one section gets a NEXT edge to the
  first chunk of the next, since their sequence ids happen to be consecutive across the boundary.
  That false continuity means a chunk window built at a section boundary pulls in unrelated context
  from the adjacent section, degrading answer quality. The fix: require matching formId AND item on
  both chunks when creating NEXT.
