The complete **end-to-end record of a single agent run** — a hierarchical tree of spans
linked by one trace ID. Because it holds full inputs, outputs, and every intermediate step, a
trace is the unit of replay, cost attribution, and evaluation at scale: you can run automated
checks over thousands of traces to detect regressions and measure quality.
