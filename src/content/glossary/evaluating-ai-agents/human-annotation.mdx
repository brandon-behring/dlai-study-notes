Evaluation by **human labellers or end-user feedback**. The most accurate technique and the
standard for safety-critical or genuinely ambiguous judgments, but the slowest to scale and
subject to selection bias. It is the fallback when neither code-based checks nor an LLM judge
are reliable enough — and the source of the ground-truth labels the other techniques are
measured against.
