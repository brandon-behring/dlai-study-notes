term: Ship Decision
aliases: [shipping decision, variant promotion]

see: [dashboard-comparison, statistical-significance]
tags: [evaluation, production]
---

Deciding whether to promote a candidate variant by reading the **whole per-evaluator row plus
cost, latency, and statistical significance** — never an aggregate score alone. A correctness
gain that isn't statistically significant, or that a latency SLO would reject, is not a ship.
The dashboard supports the call; cost/latency/significance are the context that turns scores
into a decision.
