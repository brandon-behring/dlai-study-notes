3–5 labelled example judgments added to a judge prompt to **fix calibration**, especially on
boundary cases (case-insensitive match, floating-point tolerance, polite-but-wrong responses).
One of the four judge-improvement levers. In a self-improving pipeline these are drawn from
collected production data — which is exactly why feedback poisoning can corrupt the judge
through them.
