The deviation that fires a production alert — commonly about **>10% from a metric's baseline**,
set per metric (evaluator scores, convergence, LLM-calls/query, p50/p99 latency, cost/query).
The tuning trade-off: too tight and the alert is noisy and ignored, too loose and a silent
regression slips past. Thresholds turn the monitored time-series into actionable signals.
