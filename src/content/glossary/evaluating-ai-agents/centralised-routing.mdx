---
term: Centralised Routing
aliases: [central router]
domain: eval-foundations
see: [distributed-routing, router]
tags: [agents, routing]
---

A routing architecture where a **single router makes every decision**. Because all routing
flows through one decision point, it is the easiest to trace and to evaluate — but that
single point becomes a bottleneck for complex, branching flows. Contrast with
[distributed routing](#term-distributed-routing), which trades evaluation simplicity for
flexibility.
