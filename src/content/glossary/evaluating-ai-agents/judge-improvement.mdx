The four levers for raising a failing judge's agreement rate, each targeting a different
failure mode: **prompt engineering** (misunderstood criteria — ~80% of judge failures),
**few-shot examples** (calibration on boundary cases), **model selection** (capability gaps),
and **semantic similarity** (false negatives on paraphrases). Apply one at a time and re-run
meta-evaluation after each — the agreement rate is the single metric that says whether it worked.
