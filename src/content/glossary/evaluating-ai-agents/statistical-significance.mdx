---
term: Statistical Significance
aliases: [significance]
domain: trajectory-evals
see: [ship-decision, dashboard-comparison]
tags: [evaluation, statistics]
---

Whether a measured score difference between variants is **real or sampling noise** — acutely
relevant on the small "comprehensive, not exhaustive" datasets used for fast iteration. A
higher aggregate score with no significance is not a reason to ship: gather more data or run an
A/B test first. It is one of the contexts (with cost and latency) that an aggregate score needs
before it becomes a decision.
