---
term: Meta-Evaluation
aliases: [evaluating the evaluator]
domain: llm-judge-monitoring
see: [agreement-rate, false-positive-rate]
tags: [evaluation, llm-as-judge]
---

Evaluating an **evaluator's own accuracy** by comparing its judgments against known ground
truth — quantified as the agreement rate between an LLM judge and a deterministic (code-based)
reference. Without it, a dashboard's "92%" is meaningless if the judge itself is only 75%
accurate. For subjective dimensions with no code oracle (tone, creativity), human
inter-annotator agreement replaces the code-based reference.
