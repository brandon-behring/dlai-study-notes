---
term: Convergence Score
aliases: [convergence]
domain: trajectory-evals
see: [trajectory, wasted-work-cost]
tags: [trajectory, metrics]
---

A metric in $[0,1]$ for the fraction of *completed* runs that follow the optimal,
minimum-step path across similar queries (≥ 0.8 stable, 0.5–0.8 investigate, below 0.5
unreliable). It measures **consistency, not correctness** — 1.0 can mean a consistently *wrong*
shortcut — and is blind to waste present in *every* run (the waste enters the minimum). Always
pair it with per-step correctness evaluators.
