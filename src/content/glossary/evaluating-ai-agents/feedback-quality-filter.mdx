The defenses that keep noisy feedback out of a self-improving pipeline: an **engagement-time
threshold** (discard sub-2-second clicks), **cross-signal validation** (a "helpful" click
followed by an immediate re-query of the same topic is treated as noise), **confidence
weighting** (trust users with a history over first-touch clicks), and a periodic **human
audit** of a sample. Together they are the firewall between automated feedback and dataset
quality.
