The **ordered sequence of all steps** — router decisions, tool calls, LLM completions — from
input to final response. It captures not just *what* the agent produced but *how* it got
there. Two agents can reach the same correct answer by very different paths, so the trajectory
carries the cost, latency, and failure-variance that the final answer alone hides — at scale,
the path decides viability.
