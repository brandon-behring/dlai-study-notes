The role label on a span. Open Inference defines four: **Agent** (the full run), **Chain** (a
router iteration), **Tool** (a skill invocation), and **LLM** (a single model call). The kinds
give a trace its **Agent ⊃ Chain ⊃ Tool ⊃ LLM** shape, so a reader can tell a routing step
from a tool call from a raw model call at a glance.
