The role label on a span. Open Inference defines several kinds; the four most relevant to
agents are **Agent** (the full run), **Chain** (a router iteration), **Tool** (a skill
invocation), and **LLM** (a single model call) — others include Retriever, Reranker, and
Embedding. These four give a trace its **Agent ⊃ Chain ⊃ Tool ⊃ LLM** shape, so a reader can
tell a routing step from a tool call from a raw model call at a glance.
