---
term: Alpha (α)
aliases: [LoRA alpha, scaling factor]
domain: sft-vs-rl
see: [lora, rank]
tags: [sft, lora]
---

A scaling factor on the LoRA update: $W + \frac{\alpha}{r}\Delta W$. The **ratio
$\alpha/r$** — not $\alpha$ alone — controls adaptation magnitude, so changing $r$
without rescaling $\alpha$ silently changes the effective update size. Common
default: $\alpha{=}16$ with $r{=}8$ (ratio 2).
