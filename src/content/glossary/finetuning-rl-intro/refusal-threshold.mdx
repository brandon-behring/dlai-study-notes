---
term: Refusal Threshold
aliases: [abstention threshold]
domain: evaluation
see: [ece]
tags: [evaluation]
---

A confidence cutoff (illustratively ~0.7 — no canonical value) below which a model should
abstain rather than risk a hallucination. Tuned per domain by risk tolerance, trading **over-refusal** (refusing
safe, answerable queries — hurts UX) against **under-refusal** (answering confidently
when wrong — breaks trust).
