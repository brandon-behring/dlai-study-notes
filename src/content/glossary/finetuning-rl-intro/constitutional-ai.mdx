Anthropic's alignment method: define behavioral principles (a "constitution"),
use the model's self-critique against them to generate SFT data, then train a
reward model on **AI-generated** preference pairs for RL (RLAIF). Scales
harmlessness with far fewer human labels — but inherits the critic model's blind
spots, so human spot-checks remain essential.
