---
term: Cold-Start SFT
aliases: [cold start]
domain: production-pipelines
see: [r1-zero, rejection-sampling, sft]
tags: [sft, production]
---

An initial supervised fine-tune on a small, high-quality set of reasoning traces, run
**before** RL to give the model stable formatting and consistent language. It avoids the
language-mixing and formatting instability of pure-RL R1 Zero — the first *consolidation* step
in the explore↔consolidate rhythm of a production pipeline.
