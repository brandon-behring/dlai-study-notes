Reinforcement Learning from **AI** Feedback — train the reward model on AI-generated
preference labels instead of human ones. Constitutional AI pioneered it; the paradigm has
since grown to **self-play** preference generation (a model labels its own outputs —
Meta's Self-Rewarding LMs), **principle-conditioned** generation (condition on specific
principles per query), and binary-feedback simplifiers that cut $O(n^2)$ pairwise
comparisons to $O(n)$ labels. Cheaper and more consistent than human feedback, but needs
calibration against human preference to avoid compounding self-bias.
