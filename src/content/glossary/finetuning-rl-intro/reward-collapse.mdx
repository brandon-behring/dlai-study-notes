The production-monitoring face of **reward hacking**: the model finds a degenerate strategy
that scores high on the *automated* reward while producing poor outputs — so your dashboards
look fine while users complain. Tells: reward plateaus or rises as user satisfaction falls,
output diversity drops, and length pads without information gain. Defense: monitor a
human-judgment metric and output diversity alongside reward, and set SLOs on **both**.
