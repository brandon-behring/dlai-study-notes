---
term: Catastrophic Forgetting
aliases: [catastrophic interference]
domain: evaluation
see: [lora, error-analysis]
tags: [evaluation, sft]
---

When training on new tasks erases previously-learned capabilities — the model "forgets"
things it used to do well. The SFT-side loss-of-generality failure (the RL-side cousin
is mode collapse). Mitigations: mix in ~1% pretraining/general data, use **LoRA** (it
caps how much the base weights move), and keep epochs low.
