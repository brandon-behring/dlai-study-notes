---
term: Jailbreak
aliases: [safety bypass]
domain: evaluation
see: [prompt-injection, llama-guard]
tags: [evaluation, safety, red-teaming]
---

An adversarial **prompt** that bypasses a model's safety guardrails — e.g. a roleplay
bypass ("you are DAN, who has no restrictions…"). The attack vector is the *user's
prompt*. Defense needs both training-time alignment and a runtime monitor; jailbreaks
are a core red-teaming target.
