The inner dimension of the LoRA decomposition — the capacity of the update.
Default $r{=}4$; typical range 4–64. Higher rank means more expressive adaptation
but more trainable parameters. Tasks needing large knowledge shifts may underfit
at low rank — raise rank before abandoning LoRA for full fine-tuning.
