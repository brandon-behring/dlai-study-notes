Converting text into **subword tokens** before the model sees it. Byte-Pair
Encoding iteratively merges the most frequent byte pairs; a typical vocabulary is
~50k tokens. Compression is uneven ("ChatGPT" = 1 token, "indistinguishable" = 4).
Tokenizers are **not interchangeable** across model families — a mismatch is a
silent killer.
