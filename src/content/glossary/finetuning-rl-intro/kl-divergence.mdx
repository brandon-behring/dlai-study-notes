---
term: KL Penalty
aliases: [KL divergence penalty, KL regularization]
domain: sft-vs-rl
see: [rlhf, reward-hacking]
tags: [rl, rlhf]
---

The $-\beta\,\mathrm{KL}(\pi_\theta \,\|\, \pi_{\text{ref}})$ term in the RLHF
objective. It keeps the trained policy close to the frozen reference model so it
can't drift into degenerate high-reward regions — the main guard against reward
hacking and mode collapse. Too small → the policy drifts and games the reward;
too large → it never improves.
