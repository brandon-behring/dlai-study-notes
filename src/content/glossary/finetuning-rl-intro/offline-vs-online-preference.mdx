---
term: Offline vs Online Preference Learning
aliases: [offline RL, online RL]
domain: data-and-grading
see: [dpo, ppo, grpo]
tags: [rl]
---

A data-flow distinction. **Offline** methods (DPO) train on a *fixed* preference set
collected once. **Online** methods (PPO, GRPO) score *freshly generated* rollouts against
the current policy in real time. Online is more expensive but avoids the distribution
shift between stale data and a policy that has since moved — the reason on-policy methods
often reach higher ceilings despite the extra cost.
