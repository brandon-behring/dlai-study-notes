---
term: LLM-as-Judge
aliases: [model-graded eval]
domain: data-and-grading
see: [reward-model, reward-hacking]
tags: [evaluation]
---

Using a (typically stronger) language model to score outputs from the model
being trained or evaluated, replacing or supplementing human annotation.
Scalable, but can drift, be gamed, and is biased toward verbose, confident
answers — so its trustworthiness as an RL reward is contested.
