term: PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization)

The RL algorithm behind ChatGPT's RLHF. Uses a **clipped** surrogate objective —
the probability ratio $\pi_\theta/\pi_{\text{old}}$ is capped near 1.0 (typically
$\epsilon{=}0.2$) — to prevent destructively large updates, plus a learned
**critic** for token-level credit assignment (GAE). The critic is the 4th model
of RLHF.
