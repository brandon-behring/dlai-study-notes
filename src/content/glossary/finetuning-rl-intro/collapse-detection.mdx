---
term: Collapse Detection
aliases: [diversity filtering, mode-collapse detection]
domain: data-and-grading
see: [synthetic-data, rejection-sampling, catastrophic-forgetting]
tags: [data, filtering]
---

Flagging and removing near-identical synthetic outputs that signal the generator has
fallen into a repetitive mode (measured by embedding similarity or deduplicated response
prefixes). It is **essential, not optional**: without it a "diverse" dataset can hide
thousands of memorizable near-duplicates that inflate apparent volume while teaching
almost nothing. Distinct from *catastrophic forgetting*, which is about losing prior
capability, not duplicate generation.
