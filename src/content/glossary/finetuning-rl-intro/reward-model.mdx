---
term: Reward Model
aliases: [RM, preference model]
domain: data-and-grading
see: [llm-as-judge, reward-hacking, rl]
tags: [rl]
---

A model trained to predict human (or AI) preference scores for LLM outputs,
providing the scalar reward that drives policy optimization in RLHF. Can be a
dedicated model or an LLM-as-judge. A weak or gameable reward model is the usual
root cause of reward hacking.
