term: HELM (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models)
aliases: [HELM, holistic benchmark]

see: [pass-at-k, ece]
tags: [evaluation, benchmarks]
---

Stanford's benchmark covering many scenarios across **multiple dimensions** — accuracy,
calibration, robustness, fairness, bias, toxicity, efficiency — rather than a single
score. Useful for regression-testing a model across post-training iterations: a change
that lifts accuracy but worsens calibration or toxicity is visible instead of hidden in
one aggregate number.
