---
term: Trajectory (Rollout)
aliases: [rollout]
domain: sft-vs-rl
see: [grpo, advantage, verifier]
tags: [rl]
---

In RL post-training, one complete sample of (input, model-generated output, reward).
The model generates its own output — the **rollout** — and a verifier or reward
model assigns a scalar reward. RL learns from trajectories it produces itself
(trial-and-error), which is why it needs an online generation loop, unlike SFT's
fixed (input, target) pairs.
