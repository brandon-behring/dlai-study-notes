---
term: Base Model
aliases: [pretrained model, foundation model]
domain: post-training-foundations
see: [pre-training, post-training]
tags: [post-training]
---

A language model after pre-training only, before any post-training. It predicts
the next token well but has no notion of instruction-following, safety
compliance, or structured reasoning — which is why it cannot be deployed as a
chat assistant as-is.
