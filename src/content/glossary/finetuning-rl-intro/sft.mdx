---
term: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
aliases: [SFT, instruction tuning]
domain: sft-vs-rl
see: [rl, chain-of-thought]
tags: [sft]
---

Post-training via supervised learning on (input, desired-output) pairs: the
model imitates high-quality demonstrations. Stable and data-efficient (LoRA
makes it single-GPU feasible), but **bounded by demonstration quality** — it
cannot exceed what it was shown.
