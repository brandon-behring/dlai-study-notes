---
term: Chinchilla Scaling Law
aliases: [Chinchilla rule, compute-optimal scaling]
domain: data-and-grading
see: [pre-training, sft-data-scale]
tags: [scaling, pretraining]
---

Compute-optimal **pre-training** uses roughly **20 tokens per parameter** — a 7B model
wants about 140B tokens. It sets the *pre-training* data appetite and is a frequent
interview prompt. Post-training ignores it: it saturates with **thousands** of curated
examples, not billions of tokens. Note that most production models are now deliberately
trained *beyond* Chinchilla-optimal, because extra data keeps paying off at inference time.
