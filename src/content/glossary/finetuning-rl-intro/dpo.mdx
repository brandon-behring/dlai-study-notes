---
term: DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)
aliases: [DPO]
domain: sft-vs-rl
see: [ppo, grpo, bradley-terry]
tags: [rl, dpo]
---

Trains the policy **directly** on `chosen`/`rejected` preference pairs, skipping
the separate reward model and the online RL loop, with an implicit KL term
(strength $\beta$) to a reference. **Offline**: one fixed dataset, trained once —
contrast PPO/GRPO, which generate fresh rollouts online. Lightweight and stable;
the Qwen-style alternative to PPO.
