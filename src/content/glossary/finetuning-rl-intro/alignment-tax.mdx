---
term: Alignment Tax
aliases: [reward-preference gap, alignment gap]
domain: evaluation
see: [reward-hacking, kl-divergence]
tags: [evaluation, rl]
---

The gap between a model's automated **reward score** and its actual **human-preference**
quality. A *growing* alignment tax during RL is the key Goodhart's-Law diagnostic: the
model is optimizing the proxy (reward) without improving the true objective. Tracked
alongside KL divergence and rollout diversity as an RL health signal.
