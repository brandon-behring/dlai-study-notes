---

All training after pre-training that aligns a base model with human intent —
supervised fine-tuning (SFT), reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF),
direct preference optimization (DPO), and related techniques. It turns a
next-token predictor into a model that follows instructions, refuses unsafe
requests, and reasons in structured steps.
