---
term: Expected Calibration Error (ECE)
aliases: [ECE, calibration error]
domain: evaluation
see: [refusal-threshold, pass-at-k]
tags: [evaluation, metrics]
---

How well a model's expressed confidence matches its actual accuracy. Bin predictions by
confidence; ECE is the weighted average of $|\text{accuracy} - \text{confidence}|$ per
bin (lower is better). Critical wherever users (or downstream systems) trust the model's
confidence signal — a confidently-wrong model is worse than an uncertain one.
