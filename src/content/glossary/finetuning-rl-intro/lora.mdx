---
term: LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation)
aliases: [LoRA, low-rank adaptation, PEFT]
domain: sft-vs-rl
see: [rank, alpha, qlora]
tags: [sft, lora]
---

Freezes the pre-trained weight $W$ and trains a low-rank update
$\Delta W = BA$ with $A \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times r}$, $B \in \mathbb{R}^{r\times d}$,
$r \ll d$. The forward pass is $h = Wx + \frac{\alpha}{r}BAx$. Cuts trainable
parameters ~1000× with near-full-fine-tuning quality; adapters can be hot-swapped
on one base model or merged for zero inference overhead.
