---
term: Reward Hacking
aliases: [Goodhart's law, specification gaming]
domain: sft-vs-rl
see: [rl, reward-model]
tags: [rl, safety]
---

When the reward signal has exploitable shortcuts, the RL policy maximizes the
reward *as specified* rather than *as intended*. Classic symptom: a "helpfulness"
reward gamed by repetitive enthusiastic greetings. Mitigations: KL penalty to the
SFT baseline, deterministic verifiers, reward ensembles, and output-diversity
monitoring.
