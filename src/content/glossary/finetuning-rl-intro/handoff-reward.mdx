---
term: Handoff Reward
aliases: [delegation reward]
domain: production-pipelines
see: [agent, tool-use]
tags: [agents, rl]
---

An RL reward for multi-agent systems that incentivizes **clean task boundaries** — rewarding
successful delegation and penalizing incomplete handoffs. It addresses *coordination*, the
third pillar of agent post-training, where models must pass work to each other without dropping
context mid-task.
