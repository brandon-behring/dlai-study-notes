term: Teacher Forcing
aliases: []

During training the model receives the **ground-truth** previous tokens as input
at each step — not its own predictions. Because every position then has a known
context, loss can be computed for **all positions in a single parallel forward
pass**, instead of sequentially. This is why SFT is orders of magnitude faster
than autoregressive RL rollouts.
