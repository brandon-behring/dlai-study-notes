Training data generated by language models rather than collected from humans. A pipeline
combines four operations — **generate** (templates, varied temperature, personas),
**filter** (the critical step), **transform** (restyle, adjust difficulty), and **score**
(multi-axis quality). The defining insight: filtering matters more than generation,
because it is easy to generate synthetic data and *dangerously* easy to generate bad
synthetic data.
