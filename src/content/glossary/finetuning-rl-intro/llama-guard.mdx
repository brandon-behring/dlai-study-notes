Meta's safety-classification model: given a prompt–response pair it returns a
structured `safe` / `unsafe\nS{code}` verdict across a fixed harm taxonomy. Used
to evaluate model safety at scale instead of relying solely on human reviewers.
As of 2026-06, Llama Guard 4 (12B, multimodal) spans 14 categories (S1–S14).
