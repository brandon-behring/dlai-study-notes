---
term: RLHF (RL from Human Feedback)
aliases: [RLHF]
domain: sft-vs-rl
see: [ppo, reward-model, kl-divergence]
tags: [rl, rlhf]
---

The pipeline that aligns an LLM to human preferences: (1) train a reward model on
preference pairs, (2) optimize the policy with RL to maximize reward **minus a KL
penalty** to a frozen reference. Full PPO-based RLHF keeps **four models** in
memory (policy, reference, reward, value/critic).
