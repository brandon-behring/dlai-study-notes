A model that **takes actions in an environment** — calling tools, planning multi-step
strategies, coordinating with other agents — rather than just emitting a response.
Post-training an agent is harder than instruction-following because the reward comes from
*task completion*, which is **sparse and delayed**, making credit assignment across a long
action sequence the central difficulty.
