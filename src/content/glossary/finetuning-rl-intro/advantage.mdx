---
term: Advantage
aliases: [advantage estimate]
domain: sft-vs-rl
see: [ppo, grpo]
tags: [rl]
---

How much better an action (or output) was than a baseline: $A = R - V$. Positive
advantage means better-than-expected (make it more likely); negative means worse.
PPO estimates the baseline $V$ with a learned **critic** (GAE, token-level); GRPO
uses the **group mean reward** as the baseline (sequence-level), avoiding the critic.
