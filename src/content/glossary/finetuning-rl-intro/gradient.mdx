With respect to the output logits, cross-entropy has the elegant form
$\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial z_i = p_i - \mathbb{1}[i=y]$ — literally
**predicted − target**. The update self-scales with error: a confident-correct
token ($p=0.95$) gets a tiny $0.05$ nudge, a wrong one ($p=0.1$) a strong $0.9$ push.
