---
term: Graph Traversal
aliases: [traversal]
domain: kg-fundamentals
see: [knowledge-graph, vector-similarity-search, multi-hop-pattern]
tags: [retrieval, querying]
---

Retrieval by **following typed relationships** from a starting node to connected nodes — the
graph analogue of adjacency. It answers "what is *connected* to this?", surfacing
connection-based context (a company's investors, a document's sections) that similarity search
misses. Cost is roughly $O(k^d)$ in the local fan-out ($k$ = avg connections, $d$ = hops),
versus $d$ self-JOINs over a whole table in SQL.
