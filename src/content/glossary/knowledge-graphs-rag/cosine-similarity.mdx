---
term: Cosine Similarity
aliases: [cosine]
domain: text-for-rag
see: [similarity-score, embedding]
tags: [vector-search, metrics]
---

A similarity metric measuring the **angle between two vectors, ignoring magnitude**:
$\cos\theta = \frac{A \cdot B}{\lVert A\rVert\,\lVert B\rVert}$, ranging −1 to 1 (Neo4j's
vector-index *score* normalises it to 0–1). It is OpenAI's recommended function, and for *normalised* vectors it
ranks results identically to Euclidean distance — so prefer Euclidean only when vector
magnitude carries meaning (e.g. raw TF-IDF). The standard choice for embedding search.
