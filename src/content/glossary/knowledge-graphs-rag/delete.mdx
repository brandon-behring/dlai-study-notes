The Cypher clause that **removes matched nodes or relationships** (returns no data). It
**cannot delete a node that still has relationships** — Neo4j protects referential integrity —
so you must delete the relationships first, or use [DETACH DELETE](#term-detach-delete) to
remove the node and its relationships together.
