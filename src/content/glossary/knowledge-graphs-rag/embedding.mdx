term: Embedding

A **dense vector representation of text** in high-dimensional space, where semantically similar
texts have high cosine similarity. OpenAI's default model outputs **1536-dimensional** vectors.
The embedding model is the *ceiling* of retrieval quality: if it encodes two related concepts
as distant vectors, no index tuning recovers the match — which is why domain-specific corpora
sometimes need fine-tuned or specialised models.
