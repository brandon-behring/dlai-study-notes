Data stored as **interconnected entities** — [nodes](#term-node) joined by typed, directed
[relationships](#term-relationship), both carrying [properties](#term-property). The "diagram
*is* the data." Its value for RAG is that retrieval can **traverse** connections rather than
rely on embedding distance alone, surfacing related entities (investors, org charts, filings)
that vector similarity cannot reach.
