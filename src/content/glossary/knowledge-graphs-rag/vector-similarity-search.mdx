---
term: Vector Similarity Search
aliases: [vector search, similarity search]
domain: kg-fundamentals
see: [graph-traversal, rag]
tags: [rag, retrieval]
---

Retrieval that encodes text as high-dimensional **embeddings** and finds nearest neighbours by
a distance metric (cosine). Fast and effective for *semantic* matching — "what text is similar
to my question?" — but it **cannot follow structural relationships** between entities. That gap
is exactly what [graph traversal](#term-graph-traversal) fills in a graph-RAG system.
