Electronic Data Gathering, Analysis, and Retrieval — the SEC's public database for corporate
filings, including Form 10-K and Form 13. It is the open access point for the raw documents that
seed a financial knowledge graph: every filing is keyed by the company's **CIK** (Central Index
Key), the identifier that links a company across all of its filings.
