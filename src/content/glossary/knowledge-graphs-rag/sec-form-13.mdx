A filing made quarterly by institutional investment-management firms reporting their holdings of
public-company stock. Each record carries the manager's identity (name, CIK, address), the
company's identity (via CUSIP), and the position (share count, monetary value, reporting quarter).
In this guide, Form 13 is the *second* dataset: connecting it to the existing Form 10-K graph
turns documents into a graph of who invests in whom, answering questions vector search alone
cannot.
