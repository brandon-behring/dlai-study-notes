The data model Neo4j implements: **nodes and relationships that both carry properties**, with
labels grouping nodes and types+direction on relationships carrying semantics. It contrasts with
the RDF *triple* model (subject–predicate–object); the property-graph model is what makes a
relationship a rich record rather than a bare link, and underlies every example in this guide.
